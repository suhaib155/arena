## 3. Extra points not in any claim
- The in-app policy (M/app/legal.tsx:10-15) says map providers "receive map-area requests to display real tiles", which is more candid about Google Maps than the website. Website lists Google Maps SDK (P13 [:59]) but not what it receives. Proposed: "Google Maps may receive the map area you are viewing and standard device and network information, under Google's own terms. We cannot see or delete that data."
- The in-app policy omits the 1-hour orphan clean-up and backups; website covers them. Policies should be one text maintained once (owner decision).
- Neither text says explicitly that wallet addresses are treated as personal data. Proposed one line in the Wallet row: "A public wallet address can be linked to you by others; we treat it as personal data."
