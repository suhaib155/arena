Common questions

The map or my route does not appear

MovenRun needs location permission while the app is open. Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → MovenRun → Permissions → Location and choose "Allow only while using the app". Precise location gives the most accurate route. Move outdoors for a better GPS signal.

I didn't get XP for a session

Movement rewards need real movement. Standing still, very short sessions, or finishing a mission early do not award movement XP. This keeps the game fair for everyone.

I didn't receive my sign-in code

Check your spam folder and wait a minute before requesting another code. Codes expire after 5 minutes.

Does MovenRun track me in the background?

No. Location is used only while a session is running in the app, or when you tap Locate me. See the privacy policy.

Are there tokens, crypto or payments?

No. This version has no purchases, payouts, tokens or wallets. XP, levels and badges are in-app progress with no cash value.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Account → Account security → Delete account, or use the account deletion page.

Move safely

Pay attention to your surroundings, traffic and the weather, and stop when you need to. MovenRun's distances and timers are game features, not medical or navigation advice.
