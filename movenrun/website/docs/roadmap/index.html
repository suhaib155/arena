Roadmap

Prove the game, put deeds on mainnet, turn the economy on, hand over the parameters. In that order, and for a reason.

Phase 1 · The movement game

Sessions, claiming, the defense and control model, districts and mastery, and the verification that makes any of it mean something. This phase answers the only question that matters early: is walking the same streets every week actually fun, and does the map reward it properly.

In this phase: movement sessions, route summaries, streaks, the territory map, district mastery, and server side route verification.

Phase 2 · Deeds on mainnet

The deed registry goes to Base mainnet with a readable supply, and held ground starts becoming deeds players hold themselves. This is the phase where MovenRun stops being a game with a plan and starts being a game with an asset layer.

In this phase: the deed registry on mainnet, published contract addresses, and deeds issued against ground already held.

Phase 3 · The live economy

$MOVE issues against verified movement on the capped schedule, tolls start paying deed holders, and the sinks come online with claiming, challenges, fortification, and gear. Challenges open, so ground can change hands.

In this phase: $MOVE issuance, tolls to holders, the challenge system, and the full set of sinks.

Phase 4 · Community governance

Emission settings, treasury allocation, and protocol parameters move to onchain proposals and voting by the people holding ground.

In this phase: onchain proposals, voting, and community control of the treasury.

How we ship

Phases move on evidence rather than on dates. The game has to be worth playing before deeds are worth holding, and verification has to be sound before it carries value. Details change as the work does, and this page tracks where things stand.
