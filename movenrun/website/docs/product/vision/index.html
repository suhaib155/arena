Vision

Make the world an ownable game, and make owning a piece of it as easy as going for a walk.

Mission

MovenRun's mission is to give real-world movement lasting value by making it the way you earn and hold territory. The physical world should be a shared board that anyone can play on and own a piece of, rather than a data set platforms harvest or a scheme that burns the people it attracts.

What we're building

A movement game where the ground you cover becomes yours. Move to capture zones, hold them, combine them into districts, and compete for cities. The design puts the ownership layer onchain on Base so that territory is an asset rather than a database row. That layer is not built: eight contracts sit on Base Sepolia, Base's test network, and no deed has been minted. The game around them works.

Who it's for

  • Everyday movers who want their walks, runs, and rides to build something that lasts.
  • Explorers and competitors who want to claim their neighbourhood and take a run at somebody else's.
  • Onchain natives who want a real asset layer under a game they actually enjoy.
  • People who have never touched crypto and should never have to.

Product pillars

  1. Ownership is the point. Territory should be an asset that compounds rather than a score that resets. Building that is the work still ahead.
  2. Movement is checked. Every claim has to be backed by activity that plausibly happened. This part is built.
  3. Crypto stays out of the way. Embedded wallets and sponsored gas are the plan, and neither exists yet.
  4. The network should belong to its players. Today one key controls every deployed contract, and moving off that is the first governance milestone.
  5. Built to last. Caps and sinks come before incentives, because the other order has been tried in public and did not end well.

Crypto, without the crypto

The biggest thing holding onchain products back is onboarding. The plan is to remove it: one tap, a wallet created for you, gas paid for you, and nothing to buy first. That plan is not built. No wallet provider has been selected and there is no gas sponsorship in the codebase.

What is true today is smaller but still worth something. You can play the whole game without a wallet, a seed phrase, or a purchase, because there is no onchain layer in the app to connect to. See Onchain, made invisible.

What MovenRun is not

  • A data broker. Movement builds your territory. It is not a product we sell about you.
  • A payout scheme. There is no promise of income, and the economy is designed around ownership and utility.
  • A walled garden. Self-custody is part of the design, once there is anything to custody.
  • A race app. Consistency and presence win here. Pace does not.
