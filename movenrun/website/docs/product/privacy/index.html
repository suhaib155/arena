Privacy & your data

A movement game runs on location, which is exactly why MovenRun is built to check your activity without keeping a record of your life.

Privacy-first by design

The design goal is to keep as little sensitive data as possible and to turn movement into proofs rather than permanent trails. What the game needs is confidence that you moved, not a searchable history of everywhere you have been. Verification is built around that.

What the game uses

  • Your route, to play. Location is read during an active session to draw your route and work out which zones you crossed.
  • A proof, to settle it. A session reduces to a compact, deterministic proof of movement, which is a fingerprint rather than a stored trail.
  • Your territory. Your holdings are kept as a record of what you hold, not as a profile of where you go.

Your control

  • The app is built to minimise what it collects and to prefer summaries over granular location data.
  • The Deed Preview in the app derives everything from local data. It never sends coordinates anywhere, and the district names it shows are fixed fictional labels rather than real places.
  • Retention and deletion controls are design commitments for as the product scales, and they are not all built.
  • Self-custody of a wallet is part of the ownership design, once there is a wallet.

What MovenRun is not

  • Not a data broker. Your movement builds your territory and is not a product sold about you.
  • No third-party ad tracking, and no selling of location profiles.
  • No inference or exposure of anyone else's precise location.
