Territory & ownership

The world is a hexagonal map, and every hex is a piece of ground waiting to be claimed. Move to capture it, come back to keep it.

What a zone is

A zone is one hexagonal cell of the real world, and it is the atom of MovenRun territory. Zones are the same for everyone: two players standing on the same corner are in the same zone, anywhere on Earth. The grid comes from the open H3 system, explained in Territory computation. Capturing a zone is designed to mint a Zone Deed you hold under your own keys, and that part has not been built.

Capturing and holding

Finishing a session claims the zones your route crossed. Your holdings show up across the map, your profile, and each zone's detail view. The more often you come back, the stronger your claim gets. Those holdings live in MovenRun's records, not on a blockchain.

The life of a zone

  • Captured to held. The zone joins your territory.
  • Held to contested. Neglect leaves a zone open to a challenge.
  • Fortified. Returning and fortifying raise a zone's defensive strength.
  • Defended. Strong zones hold. Weak ones change hands.

Districts and cities

Zones group into named districts, and districts into cities. Holding a critical mass of a district earns mastery and standing, and controlling districts is how clubs are meant to contest a city. Territory is a ladder: one street, then a district, then a city your club is fighting over. See Clubs, cities & competition.

Contesting territory

Zones are not static. The design has a held zone challenged through an onchain contest: a challenger stakes, both sides' movement is weighed, and the deed holds or moves. The challenge contract is deployed to Base Sepolia and has never been used. It also has two known gaps, since a defender who never approves the contract cannot lose a zone to it, and the scores arrive by way of an oracle signature. The mechanics are in Protocol & contracts.
