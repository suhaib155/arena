Movement & proof of activity

The session is the heart of MovenRun, and it is also where trust starts. A route that could not have happened does not count.

A session, start to finish

Tap to start and you are moving within seconds. No setup, no wallet prompt. The route draws live as you go, with distance, time, and pace kept simple so your eyes stay on your surroundings. Pause and resume whenever you need to. Finishing produces a summary and the capture moment that turns the outing into territory.

Why movement is checked

If territory is going to be worth holding, the game cannot take movement on faith. A session is validated for physical plausibility, meaning realistic speed, sane accuracy, and continuous motion, and reduced to a deterministic proof. Duplicates and replays are rejected, so the same walk cannot be claimed twice.

The remaining step, having the oracle attest that proof so a capture can settle onchain, is designed and not wired up. The full pipeline is in Movement verification.

Routines that build presence

  • Saved routes. Your favourite loops, ready to run again and reinforce what you hold.
  • Streaks. Consistency is the strongest force in the game. Showing up compounds.
  • Seasons and objectives. Recurring goals that give a week a focus.
  • Weekly recap. A clear read on your movement and how your territory changed.

Safety by design

  • The live screen is glanceable, and nothing needs a tap while you are moving.
  • No mechanic rewards speed over safety. You never need to rush a crossing to hold a zone.
  • Move where it is safe and legal to move. The game is built around presence rather than risk.
