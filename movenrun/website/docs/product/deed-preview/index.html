Digital ownership

In most games, your territory is a row in someone else's database. In MovenRun it is a row in ours, and the plan is to move it onchain so it stops being one.

What you would own

The design gives every captured zone a Zone Deed: a unique onchain asset for that piece of the map, held under your own keys. A deed the game cannot revoke, that you can hold, strengthen, defend, and transfer, with its history travelling with it.

None of that has happened. The deed contract is deployed to Base Sepolia, Base's test network, and no deed has ever been minted, by anyone, on any network.

What the app shows you today

The app has a Deed Preview showroom, and it is worth being precise about what it is. It reads your local stats, captured zones, district mastery, and route signals, and shows what a future deed might look like. It is not real ownership, not a mint, not a claim, and not a marketplace. Nothing in it is tradable, it has no rarity or market value, it earns nothing, and it gates nothing else in the game. It is read-only and deterministic, and it never touches a chain or a wallet.

Why ownership would change the game

  • It would persist. Your territory stays yours next season and next year, outside anyone's ability to reset it.
  • It would compound. Active deeds accrue standing and in-game yield and combine into districts and city power.
  • It would be portable. Ownership onchain under your keys, rather than locked inside one app's account.
  • It would be checkable. Anyone could verify what you hold and how you came by it.

Ownership without the friction

The part that would make this work for everyone is that you get it without doing anything crypto: a wallet created for you, fees covered, claiming a deed feeling like claiming a level. That depends on an embedded wallet provider and sponsored gas, and neither has been chosen or built. See Onchain, made invisible and the technical detail in Zone Deeds.
