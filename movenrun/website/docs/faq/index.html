Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what MovenRun is and how it works.

What is MovenRun?

A movement game. You walk, run, or cycle, and the hexagonal cells your route crosses become ground you hold. Keep visiting and the ground stays yours. Stop, and somebody else takes it.

Do I have to be fast?

No, and the design goes out of its way to make sure of it. Holding ground comes from returning to it rather than from pace or distance, so the strongest players are the most regular ones. A twenty minute walk on a Tuesday morning, every Tuesday morning, beats one long weekend run.

Can I lose ground I have taken?

Yes. Every held cell has a defense meter that drains while you stay away, and once it empties your control over the cell starts draining too. Visiting restores it. That pressure is the point, because ground nobody can lose is not worth holding.

What happens to my location data?

Your positions are used to check the route was plausible and to work out which cells you crossed, and then they are gone. We do not store coordinates. What is kept is the distance, the timing, the cells, and a fingerprint of the route that cannot be turned back into a path. See Movement and privacy.

What stops people faking movement?

Routes are checked on our servers, not on the phone. Speeds that imply a vehicle are rejected, positions with poor accuracy are dropped, a route needs enough points to describe continuous motion, and any route that duplicates one already claimed is thrown out. See Movement and privacy.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. The game is a game. Deeds and $MOVE arrive at launch for players who want that layer, and the map works without it.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. You start by moving, and there is nothing to purchase to play.

What is a deed?

At launch, a held cell becomes a deed you keep in your own wallet: one per cell, transferable, carrying the cell's history with it. Holding it pays you a toll when other players cross that ground. See Territory and deeds.

What is $MOVE for?

It is the asset the economy runs on. Verified movement issues it on a capped schedule that halves roughly every six months, claiming and challenging and fortifying consume it, and tolls route a share of it to the holder of the ground you moved through. The design is in Onchain.

What chain is it on?

The contracts run on Base Sepolia while the economy is tested. Mainnet at launch, with the addresses published then.

What is live today?

The game: sessions, claiming, holding and decay, districts and mastery, and the verification behind them. The economy is not live. Deeds, tolls, $MOVE, and challenges arrive with launch, in the order set out in the roadmap.
