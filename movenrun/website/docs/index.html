MovenRun

MovenRun is a movement game. You walk, run or cycle, and the ground you cover becomes territory on a map. Each session claims a cell. Coming back is what keeps it.

How a session worksWhat you press, what the screen shows, what happens when you stop. Capture and the mapThe cell grid, and what one session actually claims. Holding your groundHow a hold fades, and what returning does.

The loop

Move, and the app records your route. Save the session, and the first cell on that route you did not already hold becomes yours. Walk the same ground again and your hold on it firms up. Leave it alone and the hold fades on its own.

Pace does not come into it. A short walk you actually repeat beats a long run you do once, which is the whole point of the design.

What is in the app today

Movement sessions, capture, holding and decay, districts and mastery, XP, levels, streaks and collections all work. So does the route checking that decides whether a session counts.

Clubs and the city layer are in the app as a local preview built from seeded data. They do not sync between players yet. Points and deeds are designed and not built. Each page says which of these it is describing.
