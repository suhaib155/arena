MovenRun

MovenRun is a movement game. You walk, run, or cycle, and the ground you cover becomes territory on a hexagonal map of the real world.

How it worksThe loop, from a session to a district you hold. Territory and deedsWhat a cell is, how holding works, and what a deed adds. OnchainThe deed, toll, and supply design.

The loop

Your route passes through hexagonal cells and claims them. Returning strengthens your hold. Stay away and the hold decays until somebody else can take the cell off you. Cells build into districts, and mastering a district means genuinely frequenting the neighbourhood rather than passing through once.

Pace is not the point. A twenty minute walk you actually repeat beats a fast run you do once, which is what makes the map winnable by ordinary people. See How it works.

Movement has to be real

Territory is worth holding only if the movement behind it happened. Every route is checked for plausible speed, usable accuracy, and continuous motion, and a route that repeats one already claimed is rejected. Your coordinates are never stored. See Movement and privacy.

What comes at launch

Deeds turn a held cell into an asset you keep in your own wallet. Tolls pay the holder when another player crosses their ground. $MOVE is the asset both run on, issued against verified movement on a fixed schedule. The design is set out in Onchain, and the order of work is in the roadmap.
