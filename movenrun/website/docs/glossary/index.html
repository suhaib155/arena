Glossary

The language of the MovenRun world, defined once. Entries marked planned describe designs that have not been built.

Movement session
A recorded period of walking, running, or cycling. The fundamental action in MovenRun.
Proof of movement
A deterministic proof that a session plausibly happened, produced by the verification pipeline. Attesting it onchain is planned.
Zone
One hexagonal cell of the world map, and the atom of territory. Every location belongs to exactly one zone.
Territory
The set of zones a player holds and maintains.
Capture
Claiming the zones a verified route crossed.
Zone Deed
Planned. The onchain asset representing ownership of a zone, one per zone, held under the owner's keys, carrying provenance, defense, and yield. None has been minted.
Defense
A zone's resistance to being taken. It rises with active holding and fortification, and a challenge tests it.
Fortify
An action that strengthens a zone you hold.
Challenge
Planned. The onchain contest by which one player competes for another's held zone. The contract exists on a test network and has never been used.
District
A group of zones forming a neighbourhood, with its own mastery and role in city competition.
City war
Planned. A season-long contest between clubs for control of a city's districts.
Club
A team of players who pool territory and standing. Currently a local preview that does not sync between players.
Crew mission
A shared, team-oriented objective for a club.
$MOVE
Planned. The network's native asset, designed to be minted by verified movement, spent across the game, and burned by core actions. None has been minted.
Movement points
The in-game measure of your activity in a session. Unrelated to $MOVE.
Season
A recurring period of competition with its own objectives and standings.
Oracle
The contract holding the operator key that would attest movement proofs onchain. Deployed to Base Sepolia and not yet called.
Vault
The staking and treasury contract. Deployed to Base Sepolia and unused.
Governance
Planned. Onchain proposals and voting through which the community would control the network. One account controls it today.
Timelock
Planned. A mandatory delay between a governance decision passing and taking effect. Written, but no timelock contract is deployed.
H3
The open hexagonal grid system MovenRun uses to tile the world into zones.
Embedded wallet
Planned. A wallet created for a player automatically, so ownership works without seed phrases. No provider has been chosen.
Sponsored gas
Planned. Transaction fees paid on the player's behalf. There is no paymaster in the codebase.
Base
The Ethereum Layer 2 MovenRun is built for. The contracts are on its test network, Base Sepolia, and nothing is on Base mainnet.
