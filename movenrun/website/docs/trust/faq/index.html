Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what MovenRun is and how much of it exists.

What is MovenRun?

A movement game. You move in the real world, and the zones you cross on a global hex map become yours. The design puts that territory onchain as assets called Zone Deeds, funded by a token called $MOVE and eventually governed by the people who play. The game works. The onchain half does not exist yet.

Do I really own my territory?

Not yet. Your zones are records in MovenRun's game, and the team can change them. Zone Deeds are what would make them yours onchain, under your own keys, and no deed has ever been minted. The contract is deployed to a test network and has never been used.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No, and right now you could not use any if you wanted to. There is no wallet in the app, nothing to sign, and nothing to buy. Keeping it that easy once the onchain layer arrives is what embedded wallets and sponsored gas are for, and neither has been built. See Onchain, made invisible.

Do I have to buy anything to start?

No. You start by moving. Onboarding is a single tap and there is nothing to purchase.

What chain is it on?

Base Sepolia, chain 84532, which is Base's test network. Eight contracts were deployed there on 27 May 2026. Nothing is on Base mainnet, and the app does not talk to either.

What is $MOVE for?

$MOVE is designed as the network's asset: minted by verified movement, spent on capturing and upgrading territory, on challenges and seasons, on staking and governance, and burned by several of those actions. No $MOVE has been minted. The design is in the $MOVE economy.

How does MovenRun know I actually moved?

A session is validated for physical plausibility, meaning realistic speed, sane accuracy, and continuous motion, and reduced to a deterministic proof. Duplicates and replays get rejected. That pipeline is built. Attesting the proof onchain, which is the step that would let a capture settle, is designed and not wired up. See Movement verification.

What happens to my location data?

Sessions are reduced to compact proofs rather than kept as trails, and the app is built to hold as little as it can. See Privacy & your data.

Who controls the network?

One account. It holds admin on all eight deployed contracts and is also the oracle operator, the keeper, and the treasury, and there is no timelock deployed to slow any of that down. Onchain proposals and community control are the design, and getting off a single key is the first milestone on the way there. See Governance & the DAO.

Is everything live today?

No, and it is worth being specific. Live: movement sessions, capture, streaks, the territory map, district mastery, and the verification pipeline. A local preview: clubs, crew missions, and city competition, which do not sync between players. Not built: Zone Deeds, $MOVE, staking, challenges in the app, wallets, sponsored gas, and governance. The roadmap lays out the order.
