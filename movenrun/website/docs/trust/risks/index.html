Risk factors

Being honest about the risks is part of building this well. Here are the real ones, including the one that matters most right now.

Smart-contract security and key control

Risk: the immediate one is not a subtle bug. One account holds DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE on all eight contracts deployed to Base Sepolia, and the same account is the oracle operator, the keeper, and the treasury. There is no timelock deployed, so an admin action takes effect the instant it is sent. If that key were lost or stolen, everything the contracts govern would go with it. Internal review has also left open findings, among them a season-end payload that can be replayed.

Approach: the contracts hold nothing of value while they sit on a test network, which buys time rather than fixing anything. The work is to deploy the timelock, move admin behind it, and split the oracle, keeper, and treasury roles onto separate addresses before anything is deployed to mainnet. See Security.

Movement verification

Risk: location can be faked, and territory that a spoofing app can claim is worth nothing. Approach: plausibility checks, deterministic proofs, and deduplication, built before anything that depends on them, and expected to keep hardening as more people play. See Movement verification.

Economic balance

Risk: movement economies have failed when incentives outran utility. Approach: capped and decaying issuance, sinks and caps designed ahead of incentives, and demand woven through the game rather than bolted on. None of it has been tested by real use, and one designed sink turns out to be a treasury transfer rather than a burn. See the $MOVE economy.

Adoption and density

Risk: a territory game is best where there are neighbours to contest, and thin in a town with three players. Approach: a game that is rewarding solo, onboarding that does not filter out non-crypto users, and a city-by-city rollout that builds density before the contest layer needs it.

Regulatory landscape

Risk: rules for onchain assets vary by jurisdiction and keep changing. Approach: a design centred on utility and ownership rather than financial return, and a phased rollout that can adapt.

Location privacy

Risk: movement data is inherently sensitive. Approach: proofs rather than raw trails, data minimisation, and no third-party ad tracking. See Privacy & your data.
