Roadmap

Prove the movement loop, turn the map into a contest, bring ownership onchain, then hand the network over. MovenRun is in the first of those.

Today. The app plays and scores sessions on the device. The verification service and its Postgres store are built. Clubs are a local preview with seeded rivals. Eight contracts sit on Base Sepolia and nothing has been minted on them. No wallet provider has been chosen.

Phase 1 · Movement and capture

The foundation: fast onboarding, reliable sessions, movement that gets checked, and the capture loop that turns routes into territory. This phase is about proving the loop feels good and that movement maps cleanly onto a map worth holding.

In this phase: sessions, route summaries, streaks, the territory map, district mastery, and the verification pipeline behind them.

Phase 2 · Clubs and city competition

The map becomes a contest. Clubs, crew missions, district control, city wars, and seasons turn individual holdings into team strategy. The pieces exist in the app as a local preview, and what this phase adds is the shared server state that makes them real between players.

In this phase: clubs that sync, leaderboards, shared territory, and season-long city competition.

Phase 3 · Onchain ownership and the $MOVE economy

Ownership goes onchain. Zone Deeds become live assets under players' keys, the $MOVE economy comes online with its sinks and utility, and embedded wallets plus sponsored gas keep the whole thing out of the player's way. The contracts exist and are on a test network. The wallet and gas work has not started, and the provider decision is still open, so this phase is gated on more than an audit.

In this phase: live Zone Deeds on Base, the $MOVE economy, and the challenge and staking systems.

Phase 4 · Progressive decentralization

The network becomes its community's. Onchain governance takes on parameters and the treasury, and control moves off a single administrative key. The first steps are concrete: deploy the timelock, move admin behind it, and split the oracle, keeper, and treasury roles onto separate addresses.

In this phase: onchain proposals and voting, community treasury control, and a network that no longer depends on one key.

How we ship

MovenRun advances on evidence rather than on a countdown. Movement has to feel good before ownership goes live, and verification has to be sound before it carries anything of value. Timing and detail change as the work does, and this page reflects the current state rather than a promise.
