Zone Deeds

A Zone Deed is the onchain asset that would make a captured zone genuinely yours. The contract is deployed to Base Sepolia. No deed has ever been minted, so everything on this page describes a design.

One deed, one zone

Each captured zone maps to a single Zone Deed, identified by that zone's cell on the global grid. A deed is held under the owner's keys and is meant to behave like property inside the game: hold it, strengthen it, defend it, transfer it, with its history recorded alongside it. Today a captured zone is a row in MovenRun's database, and the deed layer is the plan for replacing that.

What a deed would carry

  • Ownership and provenance. Who holds it now, and how it changed hands to get there.
  • Defense. A strength that rises with active holding and fortification, and that a challenge tests.
  • Yield and loyalty. Active holders accrue in-game yield, weighted by how long they have held. Nobody has accrued any.
  • Composability. Deeds group into districts and city power and connect to the rest of the protocol.

Holding, defending, transferring

A held zone can be challenged. A challenger stakes to compete, both sides' scores are weighed against the defender's loyalty multiplier and any boost they have active, and the deed either holds or moves.

Calling that middleman-free would be wrong, and the gap is worth stating plainly. The losing defender has to have granted the challenge contract approval over their deeds before it can take one, so a defender who never approves cannot be beaten. The scores themselves come in through a signature from the oracle operator, which is a key the team controls. Closing both gaps is part of the work between here and a mainnet deployment. The mechanics are in Protocol & contracts.

Owned by you, managed for you

The point of deeds is that claiming one should require nothing crypto from the player, with the wallet, the gas, and the transaction handled out of sight. That depends on an embedded wallet provider and sponsored gas, and neither has been built. Until then there is no deed to claim and no wallet to hide. See Onchain, made invisible.
