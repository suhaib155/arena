Security

MovenRun has two things to protect: the people who play, and the protocol meant to hold their ownership. The first is in reasonable shape. The second has one very large open item.

Movement integrity

The first line of defense is proving the movement was real. Plausibility checks, deterministic proofs, and deduplication keep spoofed and replayed activity out, and this layer is expected to keep hardening as more people play. See Movement verification.

Account and asset safety

  • Sessions and tokens. Mobile session material is kept in the platform's secure storage rather than in ordinary app storage.
  • Location data. A session is reduced to a proof of movement rather than kept as a trail. See Privacy & your data.
  • Keys. Deeds and $MOVE are designed to sit under keys the user controls. There is no wallet in the app yet, so there is nothing to hold and nothing to export.

Protocol security

  • One key controls everything. A single account holds DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE on all eight Base Sepolia contracts and is also the oracle operator, the keeper, and the treasury. This is the top open security item, and splitting those roles is the first thing on the governance path.
  • No timelock. A timelock has been written but is not deployed, so an admin change takes effect immediately.
  • Role-based permissions. The roles exist in the contracts and are enforced. They are just all held by one address today.
  • Standards-based contracts. Built on OpenZeppelin's AccessControl, ERC-20, and ERC-721 implementations rather than hand-rolled primitives.

The contracts have known open findings from internal review, including the misnamed Great Burn and a replayable season payload, and they hold nothing of value while they sit on a test network. Both need closing before a mainnet deployment is worth discussing.

Anti-abuse

Because the economy is designed to reward activity, it also has to resist being farmed. Verification blocks fake movement today. Per-address daily caps and challenge cooldowns are written into the contracts and have not been exercised by real use.
