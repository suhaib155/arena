Movement and privacy

A game about ground has to know the movement was real. It does not have to know where you live.

Checking a route

Every route runs through the same checks before it counts for anything.

  1. Plausibility. A route has to move at speeds a person can actually move at. Anything implying a vehicle is rejected.
  2. Signal quality. Positions with poor accuracy are dropped, and a route made mostly of poor fixes is rejected rather than guessed at.
  3. Continuity. A route needs enough points, close enough together, to describe continuous motion. Teleports between two ends of a city do not pass.
  4. Uniqueness. Each route reduces to a deterministic fingerprint. Submit the same movement twice and the second one is rejected, and routes that overlap in time with one already accepted are caught the same way.

The checks run on our servers rather than on the phone, because a check that runs on the device is a check an attacker controls.

We do not store your coordinates

This is the part worth being precise about, because most location products say something softer.

A session's raw positions are used to work out two things: whether the movement was plausible, and which cells the route crossed. Then they are gone. What persists is the distance, the timing, the cells, and a fingerprint of the route. There is no latitude or longitude column in the system. Nobody at MovenRun can pull up where you went, because the data to answer that question was never written down.

The fingerprint is what makes duplicate detection possible without a stored trail. It is derived from the route and cannot be run backwards into a path.

What the game keeps

  • Your ground. The cells you hold and how strong your hold is.
  • Your sessions. Distance, duration, and which cells each one claimed.
  • Your account. Enough to sign you in and keep your progress attached to you.

What MovenRun does not do

  • No selling of location data, and no third party advertising trackers.
  • No background tracking. Location is read during a session you started, and not otherwise.
  • No inference or display of anybody else's precise position.
