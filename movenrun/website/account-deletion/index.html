Delete your MovenRun account

You can request deletion of your MovenRun account and the data stored with it at any time.

What is removed

  • Your account, and its Google sign-in (your email address) or wallet sign-in (your public wallet address) at our sign-in provider
  • All sign-in sessions on every device (they are signed out when you make the request)
  • Every verified session summary saved to your account (distance, time, map cells crossed)
  • Your account's security history

Deletion cannot be undone. After you make a request, this page and the app say “Deletion requested” or “Deletion pending” until we have verified that the removal is complete, including the sign-in record at our sign-in provider. Until then we do not treat your account as deleted. Your Google account and your wallet themselves are not affected: we only remove MovenRun's record of them. We keep only an anonymous record that an account was deleted, with no email, account id or other detail that could identify you.

Progress stored only on your phone (XP, streak, badges) is not on our servers. Deleting your account in the app also clears it from that phone; otherwise, uninstalling the app removes it.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open MovenRun and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Account security.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. The app tells you whether the deletion is requested or confirmed complete.

Option 2: on this page

Sign in the same way you sign in to the app. A wallet signature here is not a transaction and costs nothing.

This requests deletion of your account and every session saved to it. It cannot be undone.

Option 3: by email

If you can no longer sign in, write to info@movenrun.xyz with the subject "Delete my MovenRun account". Tell us how you signed in: the Google email address, or the public wallet address (never send a recovery phrase or private key; we will never ask for one). We may ask you to confirm you control that address before we act on the request.
