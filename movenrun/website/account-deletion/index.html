Delete your MovenRun account

You can permanently delete your MovenRun account and the data stored with it at any time.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email address you sign in with
  • All sign-in sessions on every device (they are signed out immediately)
  • Every verified session summary saved to your account (distance, time, map cells crossed)
  • Your account's security history

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. We keep only an anonymous record that an account was deleted, with no email, account id or other detail that could identify you. Database backups containing your data expire within {{BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS}} days.

Progress stored only on your phone (XP, streak, badges) is not on our servers. Deleting your account in the app also clears it from that phone; otherwise, uninstalling the app removes it.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open MovenRun and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Account security.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: on this page

Sign in with the email address of your MovenRun account. We will email you a six-digit code.

This permanently deletes your account and every session saved to it.

Option 3: by email

If you can no longer receive email at your account address, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my MovenRun account" from any address, telling us the account email. We will confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days, and reply when it is done.
