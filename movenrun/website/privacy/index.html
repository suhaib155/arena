Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Applies to the MovenRun Android app (package io.movenrun.app) version 1.0 and the MovenRun account service.

MovenRun is a walking and running game. It uses your location while you play to draw your route and measure your progress. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

MovenRun is developed and operated by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Summary

  • Location is used only while the app is open and you are in a movement session, or when you tap Locate me on a map. MovenRun never tracks you in the background.
  • You can play without an account. Your progress then stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in, we store your email address and a summary of each session you save. We do not store your raw GPS route on our servers.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app or on our account deletion page.

How the server handles your route. When a signed-in session is verified, the server reads the GPS points in memory to calculate distance, time and the map cells crossed, then discards the points. Only the summary in the table above is written to the database. Our servers do not log request bodies, so route points are not written to logs either.

Sharing a route image. If you choose to share a session, the app creates an image of your route on a map and passes it to the app you pick. Start and end points are hidden by default. The image is not sent to us.

Service providers

We use these providers to run MovenRun. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

  • Google Maps SDK for Android (Google LLC) displays the map. When a map is on screen, Google receives the map area being shown and standard device and network information. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Email delivery (Resend) sends one-time sign-in codes to your email address.
  • Hosting and database ({{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}) runs the MovenRun account service and stores the account data described above.
  • Google Play distributes the app and may provide us with aggregated crash and performance statistics under Google's terms.

The app contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

How long we keep data

  • Account data (email, sign-in sessions, verified session summaries) is kept until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in codes are stored only as a one-way hash, expire after 5 minutes and are deleted within a day of expiring.
  • Unsent sessions waiting for a connection are kept on your phone for up to 7 days, and are deleted when they are sent, expire, are discarded, or you sign out.
  • On your phone, game progress stays until you reset progress, delete your account in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Backups of the database are kept by our hosting provider for up to {{BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS}} days, after which deleted data no longer exists in any backup.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Account → Account security → Delete account. Or use the account deletion page, which works without the app. Deletion permanently removes your account, email address, sign-in sessions and every verified session summary. We keep only an anonymous count that a deletion happened, with nothing that identifies you.

Your choices and rights

  • Location permission is controlled in your phone's settings. You can deny it; the app then explains that a movement session needs location and you can still browse the rest of the app.
  • You can play without an account, sign out at any time, and sign out all devices from Account security.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your account data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored in Android's secure keystore. Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored on the server only as keyed hashes. No security measure is perfect; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

Children

MovenRun is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

No payments, tokens or wallets

This version of MovenRun has no purchases, no payouts, no cryptocurrency, no wallets and no digital ownership. XP, levels and badges are in-app progress with no cash value.

Changes to this policy

If we change how MovenRun handles your data, we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app before the change applies to data already collected.
