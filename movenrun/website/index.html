Our approach

A deed should reward patience.

A deed is a durable record that the ground you have walked for a year is ground you hold. It should never add a step to a morning walk or turn a route into a bet.

  • ✓The game is free and needs no wallet
  • ✓One deed per cell, a record of what you hold
  • ✓Long holding counts for more
  • ✓Ground is lost to neglect, never to another player
  • ✓No coordinates stored, ever
  • ✓No promise of financial return

Roadmap

Building the experience
in the right order.

The game has to be worth playing before anything is worth building on top of it. That ordering decides everything below.

Phase 1

The movement game

Sessions, claiming, the hold and decay model, districts and mastery, and the route checks behind all of it. In the app now, with territory as an on-device preview.

Phase 2

Clubs and city play

Territory moves to a record held on the server and clubs sync between players, so the ground you hold counts toward a shared total instead of a local preview.

Phase 3

Points

Points arrive as the in-game currency, earned by moving and spent on strengthening the ground you hold.

Phase 4

Deeds

Held ground can become deeds recorded under your own keys.
